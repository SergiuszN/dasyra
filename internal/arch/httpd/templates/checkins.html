Hours

{{range .Hours}} {{end}}

Server time. At each hour Dasyra looks at every user and writes only when she has something to say. Users who talked in the last 3 hours, or left two check-ins unanswered, are skipped. No hours turn check-ins off; reminders and digests still go out.

Silent users

Counted from the user's last message. The one check-in of a quiet user is the middle hour of the day{{if ge .QuietHour 0}}, now {{printf "%02d" .QuietHour}}:00{{end}}. After the second limit she waits until the user writes again. 0 turns a rule off.

Gate

Before a check-in, {{.GateModel}} reads what Dasyra would see and answers how likely it is that she has something new to say. Below the threshold the check-in is skipped without running Dasyra: most check-ins end with nothing to send. A failed gate lets the check-in run. Its answers are in the logs ("Check-in gate"), its cost under "Check-in gate". The model is set on the models page; only OpenRouter serves decision models.
